# Exclusions

Not copied: source or nested worktrees, vendor trees, Foundry artifacts, test binaries, temporary/build/module caches, raw stdout/stderr/event streams, command launchers, private configuration, native plans, journals, credentials, signatures, receipts, RPC captures, or live state.

The raw capture is retained externally. `references/RETAINED-RAW-RECORDS.tsv` gives capture-relative labels, SHA-256 values, byte sizes, and roles for raw records summarized here. This bundle does not claim those raw logs were published.
