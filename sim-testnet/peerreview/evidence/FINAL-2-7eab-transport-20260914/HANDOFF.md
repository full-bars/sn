# Handoff

Use this bundle to review the first closed transport refusal without conflating it with the live retry. The raw error is preserved in derived form and its closed stderr hash is recorded. Exact state fingerprints and reviewed limits are included. The closed transcript-derived probes establish only the observations listed in `probes/`; they do not identify the HTTP request that returned 429, replay the complete binding postcondition, or establish a source defect.
