# Exclusions

Not copied: test binaries, Go/module caches, raw events, stdout/stderr, command launchers, private state, plans, configuration, keys, signed receipt material, RLP, journals, live captures, and raw native-refusal inputs.

The bundle retains public-safe summaries, reviewed source-patch text, identity digests, compiled memberships, synthetic per-test outcome tables, source mappings, and checksums only.
